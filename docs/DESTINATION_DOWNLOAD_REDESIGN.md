# Destination collection, download invitation and footer

The destination carousel now uses a full-width image frame, larger destination heading and four image selectors. The outgoing image remains opaque beneath the incoming image during the 1.2-second dissolve, avoiding the cream flash produced when both layers fade simultaneously. Each destination starts approximately four seconds after the previous one, subject to visibility, focus, hover, reduced-motion and user pause settings. Images are decoded before presentation and failed sources are skipped.

The download area uses cream paper styling, matching dark ink store buttons and a separate four-image postcard gallery. Its image changes approximately every four seconds while visible and active. Both platform buttons keep the existing honest pre-release dialog; no download URL or release date has been invented.

The footer preserves the logo, GoEase name and exact slogan in a seamless leftward ribbon with additional travel messages. Its duplicate group is inert and hidden from assistive technology. The floating return button appears after the hero leaves view and exposes its label on hover or keyboard focus. Motion controls and reduced-motion settings stop the ribbon.

Changes are local only. Browser captures are in the ignored qa/redesign folder.
